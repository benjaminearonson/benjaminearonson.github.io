Lesson 1: Fraudulent messages

This is a legitimate email from PayPal. How can you tell?

Notice that it’s addressed “Dear James,” not “Dear Customer,” since PayPal knows your name.

There are no links or phone numbers. There’s no deadline or threat. It instructs you to log in by visiting the app or typing paypal.com into your browser. Scammers can’t do this because they need to bring you to their website or their call center employees.
