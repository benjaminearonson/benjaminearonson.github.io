Lesson 1: Fraudulent messages

A common type of scam is fraudulent messages: a message pretending to be something it’s not. Scammers will pretend to be loved ones, government employees, etc, and send messages on social media, as texts, and via email.

Phishing is a type of fraudulent message where the user receives an email or message that appears to be from a reputable organization, but is actually designed to extract personal information from the user.

Some of the most common disguises worn by phishing emails are communications from online payment services (banks, PayPal, Apple Pay, etc), communication from an IT department, and security or identity threat notices

The scammers would like you to click links, call phone numbers, and unknowingly divulge private information. Let’s learn how to avoid these scams.
