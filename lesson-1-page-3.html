Lesson 1: Fraudulent messages

This is an example of a phishing email. Do you notice anything suspicious?

Firstly, look at the “from” email address: <paypal_customer_support6983@hotmail.com>

Any email that’s really from paypal will be from an @paypal.com email address, not @hotmail.com. Also, if you don’t recognize the charge, there’s a good chance it’s a fake charge that’s trying to create a sense of urgency.

Never call a phone number or click a link in a suspicious email. Instead, open a new tab, and go straight to the real website–in this case, paypal.com–and log in to check your account, or call the customer service number listed on the website.
