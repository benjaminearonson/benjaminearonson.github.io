Lesson 1: Fraudulent messages

This is a fraudulent message. In particular, it is an AI-generated voice calling you. Do you see any red flags?

The phone number isn’t one that you recognize, and the name of the caller isn’t saved in your contacts.

The caller is requesting money urgently and insisting you don’t tell anyone about this. They want you to send money without thinking or consulting a loved one, and then they’ll run away with it.

Remember: Never send or spend money without doing thorough research or consulting a loved one.
