Welcome to Investment Tycoon!

In this game, you will be presented with various investment opportunities. Some will be legit and some will be scams.

Your task is to click Invest if you believe the opporunity is legit. If you believe the oppotunity is a scam, click on the parts of the page that indicate it's a scam. There will be three red flags for each scam.
