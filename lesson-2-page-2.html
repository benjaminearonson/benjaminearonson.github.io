Lesson 2: Investment scams

This is an investment scam email. Do you notice any giveaways?

The email claims the opportunity is urgent, with guaranteed returns, and zero risk. Nothing legitimate has these credentials.

Also, never send cryptocurrency to an unknown wallet address or funds to an unknown Zelle address. True investment platforms have websites and investment portals.

Remember the golden rule: if it seems too good to be true, it is.
