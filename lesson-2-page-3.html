Lesson 2: Investment scams

This is a trading platform article. Do you notice anything out of place?

Celebrities don’t endorse get-rich-quick schemes. This website is just using a famous face to add credibility to the scam.

No trading platform has guaranteed returns or zero risk. The urgency of “Only 7 spots left!” is a common tactic to get you to act without thinking.

Finally, glowing comments are often from fake accounts that exist to seem like unbiased testimonial.

Remember the golden rule: if it seems too good to be true, it is.
