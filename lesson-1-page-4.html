Lesson 1: Fraudulent messages

This is an example of a phishing text message. Do you notice anything suspicious?

Look closely at the link: www.wells-fargo-account.net. This is designed to look like the wells fargo website, but the real one is wellsfargo.com. Also, a real bank won’t send you a login link.

Notice the threat: “if you do not login and secure your account within 12 hours, we will approve the suspicious login attempt.” This is designed to create a sense of urgency, but a real company would never do this.

If you see a text like this and you’re not sure if it’s legitimate, log into the website on your own using their real web address, or call the number on the back of your credit card.
