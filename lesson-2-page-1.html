Lesson 2: Investment scams

Investment scams seek to trick the user into investing money with the promise of high returns. The investment might pose as cryptocurrency, real estate, the stock market, or others.

Scammers get your attention with fake articles, social media posts, or online ads that often encourage you to attend free events, order free materials, or watch free introductory videos to learn the secret to getting rich.

Fake investment sites allow you to deposit funds and watch your worth skyrocket, which entices you to invest more, but when you try to withdraw your earnings, you’ll be met with disappointment.

While plenty of legitimate investment opportunities exist online, it’s important to be able to distinguish them from common investment scams. Let’s have a look at how to do this.
