Lesson 2: Investment scams

This is a social media ad for a fraudulent investment platform.

The giveaways are over-promises, such as “zero risk” and “$5,000 in 7 days.” No real investment platform would make these promises, as all investments carry risk.

Also, notice the artificial urgency of the limited number of spots available. Real trading platforms don’t do this. Watch out for anything to do with Cryptocurrency. It’s unregulated and transactions are impossible to reverse, which is why scammers use it.
